I would ship the whole name in Outfit for all three environments. The app's name is a word, so it must follow the typographic law for words.

2. `short-measure` ships, and it genuinely answers Codex's objection. `kept-place` merely shows that a figure is missing, which a user could misinterpret as an error, a loading state, or an alignment issue. `short-measure` draws the exact mathematical failure that caused the refusal. By placing the inadequate depth directly beneath the void, it explicitly proves that the foundation cannot justify the weight of a printed figure.
