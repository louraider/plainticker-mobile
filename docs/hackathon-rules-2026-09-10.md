## 4. SKR bonus track — everything that is published

- Amount and form: "$10,000 in $SKR for the best SKR integration." (S6). "paid in SKR" (S12).
- Optional and separate: "SKR integration is optional, and the prize is separate from the main USDC prize pool." (S12)
- What counts: "Use SKR for in-app purchases, staking, rewards, access, or invent your own use case." (S12). Inspiration thread: S17.
- How it is declared: the submission form's long-text question "DOES YOUR APPLICATION HAVE AN SKR INTEGRATION? IF SO, HOW?" (S18).
- **Not published:** the SKR judging rubric; who judges it; whether the SKR prize is gated by the no-VC rule (Terms §9.2 restricts only USDC prizes) or by the 30-day dApp Store publication rule (Terms §9.3 applies to "any winner prize designated by the Organizer as subject to publication" — the designation is not published); whether one team can win both a USDC place and the SKR prize. Checked S1–S8, S12.
- Observation, not a rule: the Align API hackathon record carries `globalScoringCriteria` of AI 20 / SKR Integration 20 / UX 15 / UI 15 / Innovation 15 / Ecosystem Impact 15 (S11), while every published page says four equal 25% criteria (S3, S4, S7, S1 §8.1).
